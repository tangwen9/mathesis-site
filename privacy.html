Mathesis 隐私政策

一句话

Mathesis 没有账号，不做统计，不放广告，不追踪你。你的学习记录只存在这台设备上。唯一会离开设备的内容，是你在「建模大师」里写的问题，而且只在你的设备用不了 Apple 智能、并且你点了同意之后。

留在设备上的

学习进度、印章、笔记与高亮、收藏的卡片、界面语言与外观、建过的模型。它们存在 App 自己的存储里，不上传，也不与任何人共享。删除 App 就一并删除。

建模大师

在支持 Apple 智能的设备上，模型在设备上生成，不发送任何内容。

在其余设备上，生成由 DeepSeek（深度求索）在云端完成。第一次使用前 App 会征求你的同意。同意之后，你写的问题和每一轮的反馈经我们的转发服务发送给 DeepSeek；不附带账号、设备标识或任何能指向你的信息。请不要在问题里写下个人信息。

转发服务不保存对话内容。为了防止滥用，它在内存里按 IP 地址临时计数（每分钟与每天的请求数），不写入磁盘，服务重启即清空。

DeepSeek 怎样处理收到的内容，以它自己的隐私政策为准。

购买

解锁与订阅由 Apple 的 App Store 处理。我们拿不到你的姓名、邮箱或付款信息，只知道这台设备上的购买是否有效。

相册

只有当你在看图页点「存进相册」时，App 才会请求「添加照片」的权限，用来保存那一张图。App 读不到你的相册。

儿童

Mathesis 面向成年自学者，不面向儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的任何信息。

变更与联系

这份政策若有实质变化，会随 App 更新，并更新下面的生效日期。有疑问可以通过 App Store 页面上的支持链接联系我们。

Mathesis Privacy Policy

In one sentence

Mathesis has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your learning record stays on this device. The only content that ever leaves it is what you write in Modeling, and only when your device cannot use Apple Intelligence and you have agreed.

What stays on your device

Your progress, seals, notes and highlights, saved cards, language and appearance settings, and the models you have built. They are kept in the app's own storage, are not uploaded and are not shared with anyone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Modeling

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, models are generated on the device and nothing is sent anywhere.

On other devices, generation is done in the cloud by DeepSeek. The app asks for your consent before the first use. After that, the question you write and each round of feedback are sent to DeepSeek through our relay, with no account, device identifier or anything else that points to you attached. Please do not put personal information in your question.

The relay does not store the conversation. To prevent abuse it keeps a temporary in-memory count of requests per IP address (per minute and per day); nothing is written to disk and the count is cleared when the service restarts.

How DeepSeek handles what it receives is governed by its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Unlocks and subscriptions are handled by Apple's App Store. We never receive your name, email or payment details; the app only learns whether a purchase is valid on this device.

Photos

Only when you choose to save a figure does the app ask for permission to add photos, in order to save that one image. The app cannot read your photo library.

Children

Mathesis is made for adult self-learners. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in substance it will be updated with the app, together with the effective date below. Questions can be sent through the support link on the app's App Store page.
