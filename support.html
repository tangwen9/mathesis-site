购买没有生效

打开「今日」右上角的 ✦，在设置里点「恢复购买」。换了设备也是同样的办法，需要登录购买时用的 Apple 账户。

取消订阅

在 iPhone 的「设置 → 你的名字 → 订阅」里取消。永久档是一次性付费，没有续订。

建模大师用不了

支持 Apple 智能的设备在本机生成，不需要联网。其余设备由云端生成，需要联网，并且有次数：免费体验共 3 次，解锁之后每月 60 次。

内容纠错

每个概念页底部都列着参考文献。发现定义、定理或史料有误，请写明是哪个概念的哪一段，我们核对后更正。

隐私政策

Mathesis Support

For problems, corrections or suggestions, write to: fourier_chaplin@icloud.com

A purchase did not take effect

Tap ✦ at the top right of Today and choose Restore purchases in Settings. The same works on a new device, signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Cancelling a subscription

Cancel under Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. The lifetime option is a single payment and does not renew.

Modeling is unavailable

Devices that support Apple Intelligence generate models on the device with no connection needed. Other devices use the cloud, which needs a connection and has limits: three free trial rounds in total, then sixty rounds a month once unlocked.

Corrections

Every concept page lists its references at the bottom. If a definition, theorem or historical claim is wrong, tell us which concept and which passage and we will check and correct it.
